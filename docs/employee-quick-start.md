# Employee Quick Start — Stars PI Time Clock

## Log in

1. Go to the app URL and you'll land on the sign-in page.
2. **First time?** Click "New here? Create an account," enter your name, email, and a password (6+ characters), then click **Create account**. You're in immediately — no email confirmation needed.
3. **After that**, just enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.

## Clock in / Clock out

On your dashboard, the **Time Clock** card shows whether you're currently clocked in or out, plus your last punch time.

- If you have a shift scheduled or assigned today, a dropdown lets you pick which one you're clocking in for (or "General (no site)" if it's not tied to a specific shift).
- Click **Clock In** when you start work.
- Click **Clock Out** when you're done.

**About location:** if the shift you pick is tied to a client site, your browser will ask for location permission when you clock in/out — this checks that you're actually at the site. Allow it if prompted. If you're outside the site's radius, or your device couldn't get a location at all, the punch still goes through — it's just flagged for your admin to glance at, not blocked. Shifts with no site attached never ask for location at all.

That's it — the button and badge update immediately. If you forget to clock out, let your admin know so they can correct it; every correction is logged with a reason, so nothing gets silently changed.

## Claim a shift

1. Click **Shift Board** from the dashboard (or go to `/shifts`).
2. Browse the open shifts — each shows the date, time, and role needed.
3. Click **Claim Shift** on any one you want. Your claim goes to "pending" until an admin approves it — you'll see a badge on the shift showing your status (pending review, approved, or denied).
4. More than one person can claim the same shift before it's decided; the admin picks who gets it, so claiming isn't a guarantee.

## Check your schedule

Click **My Schedule** from the dashboard (or go to `/schedule`) to see every shift you're on — shifts you claimed from the Shift Board show as "Claimed," and shifts your office manager put you on directly show as "Scheduled."

## View the full Weekly Schedule

Click **Weekly Schedule** from the dashboard (or go to `/weekly-schedule`) to see the whole team's preplanned schedule for the week, grouped by day — this is the regular schedule your office manager/HR sets, separate from the Shift Board's day-to-day open shifts.

## Check your hours

Click **My Timesheets** from the dashboard (or go to `/timesheets`) to see your regular, overtime, and total hours for each pay period, along with its status (pending review, approved). Hours appear here once your admin runs the calculation for that pay period — they won't show up in real time as you clock in/out.

## Add it to your phone's home screen (optional)

This app works like a native app once installed:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share icon → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu → **Add to Home Screen** / **Install app**.

You'll get an icon you can tap straight to the time clock — no browser address bar to navigate.

## Signing out

Click **Sign out** in the top-right corner of the dashboard.

## Questions or something looks wrong?

If your hours look off or you're missing a punch, don't try to fix it yourself — flag it to your admin. Every punch correction requires a reason and is logged, which protects you as much as anyone if there's ever a pay dispute.
